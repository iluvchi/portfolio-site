August 28, 2026

Whenever I design and build a website, I can't stop. It may be my favorite form of design. I think about design vs. art a lot, and how the two are not seen as antitheses of each other enough.

The hardest thing about making art is thinking that it is as structured as design. The hardest thing about design is thinking that it is as creative as art. And honestly, I'm more of a hardwired designer than I am an artist, so making art soothes me because I use my brain in a way that is completely different than what I'm used to. I hope that kind of thinking becomes more natural to me so I can challenge myself further…

August 18, 2026

Today, I went to the department store after work, to purchase a Baby-G watch. After a long and hard work day, going on this venture made me feel like a proper salarywoman. Getting off at the Union Square stop and walking through the tall buildings made me feel like I was in Ginza for a second. My new watch is black with a pink panel, and has a pixel dog, cat, or dolphin that shows up when you press the watch. I can't help but wonder how many other women find solace in the subtle playfulness of the everyday accessory. Now, when I have a rough moment at work, I find myself pressing the watch just to see my pixel cuties.
